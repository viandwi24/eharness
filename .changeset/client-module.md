New subpaths `eharness/client` and `eharness/client/react` (spec 27, ADR-0059), the browser counterpart of `eharness/http`, lifted from the team example's web app. `createHarnessClient` is a typed `fetch` client of the routes (`HarnessHttpError` with `isAlreadyAnswered` / `isBusy` / `isDuplicate`); `createHarnessTransport` builds on AI SDK's `DefaultChatTransport` (last message only, `Idempotency-Key`, `202` as an empty stream plus `onQueued`, a duplicate of an active turn resumes it, replaying resume); `createChatSession` is the framework-neutral logic around a chat (history paging, follow a running turn with retry, steer while busy, stalled-connection check, interrupted-turn Continue/Retry, error wording) driving a `ChatBridge` that `@ai-sdk/svelte` `Chat` satisfies and `@ai-sdk/react` `useChat` satisfies through `chatBridgeOf`; `createActivityFeed` is an SSE reader with reconnect, `Last-Event-ID`, idle watchdog, health pings that detect a restarted server (boot id), polling fallback and dedupe; `createAttentionList` is an optimistic approvals inbox with a generation guard. Stores follow the Svelte store contract and work with `useSyncExternalStore`. `eharness/client/react` takes React by injection (`createReactBindings(React)`), no peer dependency. The client imports only `ai` at runtime; `check:imports` and a post-build check (`scripts/check-client-bundle.ts`, part of `check:package`) enforce it.
